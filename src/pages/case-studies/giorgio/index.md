<div field="title">

About the project

</div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

<div record-type="127">

<div>

<div>

<div>

<div field="text">

<div customstyle="yes">

<a href="https://www.luxury-giorgiomagnani.com/" style="font-size: 18px;">Giorgio Magnani Luxury</a> — the luxury line of Romagna Furs, which was founded in 1971 by Italian Giorgio Magnani and is currently one of the main players in the niche of premium natural fur.  

The company's factory is located in the Republic of San Marino, annually produces about 2000 models for three lines of Romagna Furs, Gimas and Giorgio Magnani, each of which is designed for its own segment from refined classic premium fur coats to bolder designs for a youth audience.  

</div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

<div record-type="127">

<div>

<div>

<div>

<div field="text">

<div customstyle="yes">

*In Russia, the brand is represented by a single store (located in St. Petersburg, initially on Konyushennaya Street), wealthy people come here from all over the country, including from Moscow.*

</div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

<div record-type="127">

<div>

<div>

<div>

<div field="text">

<div customstyle="yes">

**The duration of the advertisement:** from May 2019 to the present day.  

**Audience (gender):** women 80%, men 20%. Usually women choose their own gift – they just know better what kind of gift they need :) But still, sometimes a man can see an advertisement that will hook him – and tell his wife about it.  

**Audience (geography):** residents of the Petrogradsky, Central and Admiralteysky districts in St. Petersburg (the main audience lives there). And also the whole of the Russian Federation with an emphasis on people with a high (highest) income level + very pointwise tuned to the whole world.  

**Goal:** to increase brand awareness. Instagram is the main platform for attracting people to the boutique (since the site is not optimized for promotion). Also, the audience is used to learning about new products through Instagram.  

</div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

<div record-type="30">

<div>

<div>

<div field="title">

Challenge

</div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

<div record-type="106">

<div>

<div>

<div>

<div field="text">

<div customstyle="yes">

We decided to change the approach that is usually offered in these (rather complex and expensive) niches. We refused to mindlessly waste budgets on a huge amount of advertising in all kinds of media (although it was also, just before the opening). We abandoned offline advertising at the first stage (although at the moment it is already quite possible to launch this direction).  

*We needed to show super-efficient budget spending – both to ourselves, because we wanted to prove that we could work effectively even in such a difficult niche, and to the brand to show that we can do much more serious things.*  

</div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

The essence of the strategy

</div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

<div record-type="106">

<div>

<div>

<div>

<div field="text">

<div customstyle="yes">

We took the content from the profile as a basis (fortunately there was plenty to choose from). Thus, they promoted posts, "becoming familiar" with potential customers.  

The strategy worked on several levels at once:

<div field="title">

1 level:

</div>

</div>

</div>

<div record-type="106">

<div>

<div>

<div>

<div field="text">

<div customstyle="yes">

Such an expensive product is a luxury. In general, it looks like Apple products, only in a different market. You need to **SEE** to **WANT TO**. Even more than that:

</div>

</div>

</div>

<div field="title">

Idea

</div>

</div>

</div>

<div field="text">

**You have to SEE to WANT!**

</div>

</div>

<div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

<div record-type="795">

<div field="title">

2 level:

</div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

<div record-type="106">

<div>

<div>

<div>

<div field="text">

<div customstyle="yes">

<span>A person, constantly meeting with a product in Insta in his feed, receiving visual information, wants to make a purchase more and more. And since we flash in the tape quite often, it is WE who capture the attention of a person.</span>

</div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

<div record-type="106">

<div>

<div>

<div>

<div field="text">

*There are no options left, from whom to buy. There are no competitors left. There is only us. In a simple analogy, you don't think Apple or Samsung, you think which iPhone model you have enough money for. It's the same here.*

</div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

<div record-type="795">

<div>

<div>

<div>

<div field="title">

3 level:

</div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

<div record-type="106">

<div>

<div>

<div>

<div field="text">

<div customstyle="yes">

<span>Recognition began to disperse itself. As long as you see ads once or several times a week, nothing happens. You spent exactly as much – how many people you reached and how many impressions you made.</span>

</div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

<div record-type="106">

<div>

<div>

<div>

<div field="text">

*1000 impressions = ~150 rubles. Out of 1000 impressions, a maximum of 100 people will get acquainted with the record, go to the profile or write to Yandex.Direct (search engine like Google) no more than 10. And that's it!*

</div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

<div record-type="106">

<div>

<div>

<div>

<div field="text">

<div customstyle="yes">

But if we start showing our ads more often? There is an effect of the fact that we are **everywhere** – even if only on Instagram. You see us EVERY DAY. Several times. Imagine that instead of different ads and different posts in the feed, every fourth post would be an advertisement for one brand.

</div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

<div record-type="106">

<div>

<div>

<div>

<div field="text">

*You would be at least surprised – these are their budgets! Of course, there are no extra budgets, you just need to know how to show ads to exactly those who we need, without spending the budget on everyone else!*

</div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

<div record-type="106">

<div>

<div>

<div>

<div field="text">

<div customstyle="yes">

<span>And at this moment, really interesting things happen. Recognition begins to grow exponentially, to adjust ITSELF. A person is a social being, and lives in the need to share non-standard events of his life with others. What is considered non-standard? For example, brand advertising, which you see all the time!</span>

</div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

<div record-type="106">

<div>

<div>

<div>

<div field="text">

*At least you will tell your friend/girlfriend that Instagram is behaving strangely, or that someone seems to have bought all the ads :) "And who?" they will ask. "Yes, these, Giorgio, what's their name, on M, with expensive beautiful fur coats. Here is their profile"*

</div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

</div>

<div record-type="106">

<div>

<div>

<div>

<div field="text">

<div customstyle="yes">

Yes, this is word of mouth, but it started working even **before the sale**. This can be done only when advertising for recognition works correctly.
