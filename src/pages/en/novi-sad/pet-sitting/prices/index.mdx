# How Much Does Pet Sitting and Boarding Cost in Novi Sad in 2026?

**A current individual listing for cat care in Novi Sad starts at 500 RSD.** The supplied research snapshot also recorded some nearby private visits at 500–800 RSD, while established services charge up to 2,500 RSD depending on location. Cat Place publishes rates of 1,500–2,500 RSD per home visit. With us, Anna and Ivan, a standard visit to a cat or dog costs 1,500 RSD in our regular service area. Overnight boarding is a different service and is normally priced per night or day. We compared published local rates as of **November 22, 2026** and worked out example totals for common trips.

## How much does a cat sitter charge for a home visit in Novi Sad?

If your cat stays in their familiar home, a sitter can come by to feed them, refresh the water, clean the litter box, play with them and check that everything is fine. The price usually depends on **distance, visit duration, number of daily visits and any extra care**.

Published examples:

- **Cat Place:** one visit costs **1,500 / 2,000 / 2,200 / 2,500 RSD**, depending on which of its four zones you live in. A package of **two visits in one day** costs **2,500 / 3,000 / 3,200 / 3,500 RSD per day**. Two visits therefore do not necessarily cost twice as much as one. [2026 price list](https://www.catplace.rs/cenovnik/).
- **Individual Novi Sad cat-care listings:** the category currently shows an offer from **500 RSD**. The supplied research snapshot also recorded private visits at **500–800 RSD** nearby, **1,000 RSD** farther away and **1,200–1,500 RSD** in more distant parts of the city. These are changeable individual offers, not a market average. [Local cat-care listings](https://www.halooglasi.com/kucni-ljubimci/cuvanje-macaka/novi-sad).
- **GetManov:** **1,500 RSD per visit** in our green zone or **2,000 RSD** in the extended yellow zone. A visit usually lasts around 30–60 minutes. We send photos and videos and tell you how your pet is doing. The standard price covers up to three pets at the same address. [In-home pet sitting in Novi Sad](/en/novi-sad/pet-sitting/home-visits/).

**Example:** one cat, seven days, one standard-zone GetManov visit each day: **7 × 1,500 = 10,500 RSD**. Two visits a day: **21,000 RSD**. One daily visit in the extended zone: **14,000 RSD** for seven days. Choose the visit schedule around your cat's actual needs, not just the lowest possible price.

## How much does a dog sitter's home visit cost?

First clarify the service. **Coming in to feed and spend time with your dog**, **a home visit that includes a walk**, and **a walk-only appointment** are not the same thing.

Our standard dog visit costs **1,500 RSD**. A dog visit with a walk of around 30 minutes costs **2,000 RSD**. The extended zone adds **500 RSD per visit**. One, two or three daily visits can be arranged. [In-home pet sitting in Novi Sad](/en/novi-sad/pet-sitting/home-visits/).

Walk-only offers may be cheaper: Dog Walker Novi Sad publishes **300 RSD for 30 minutes, 350 RSD for 45 minutes and 400 RSD for an hour**. This is a separate walk-only price list, not a complete home-care visit; it is undated, so confirm current availability and prices. [Dog Walker](https://dogwalkerns.wixsite.com/dwns/cene).

For a dog staying home over several days, plan for their normal walks, routine and human company. A couple of brief visits may not be a suitable substitute for boarding.

## How much does cat boarding cost in Novi Sad?

There are three main options: **your cat stays home and receives visits**, **your cat stays in a sitter's home**, or **your cat boards in a specialist cat hotel**. The lowest nightly rate is not always a like-for-like comparison: a shared room, private room and home environment are different experiences.

### Cat Place: room type and season

|---|---:|---:|---:|

| Private room | 2,000 | 2,200 | 2,500 RSD/night |

It publishes a 20% accommodation discount for a second and subsequent cat from the same household, and separate long-stay discounts that cannot be combined. [Official Cat Place pricing](https://www.catplace.rs/cenovnik/).

### What if you have two or three pets?

Some sitters charge per animal; others offer household rates. Our **home boarding for two pets from the same family** costs **3,000 RSD/day** for 1–3 days or **2,500 RSD/day** for 4–13 days. For **home visits**, the standard rate covers up to **three pets at the same address** if their care doesn't require substantially more time. Compare the total for your household, not just the advertised single-pet rate.

## Which costs less: home visits or boarding?

**Cheaper does not automatically mean better for your pet.** One daily cat visit can cost less than a private hotel room, but more than a shared hotel room. For dogs, the need for walks and company may make repeated home visits less practical than boarding.

Examples using published prices, without add-ons or special circumstances:

|---|---|---:|

## What extra fees should you ask about?

Check the complete price rather than just the headline “from” rate:

- **Travel distance:** Cat Place uses four home-visit zones; GetManov uses a green base zone and a yellow extended zone (+500 RSD per visit).
- **Visit frequency:** two visits may be sold as a daily package or billed as two separate appointments.
- **Dog walking:** may not be included in a standard home visit.
- **Holidays and seasons:** Cat Place changes hotel pricing by season; Dogs Empire lists a holiday rate.
- **Food and medication:** Cat Place has published surcharges; discuss specialized care directly.
- **Transport and collection time:** pet taxi, late check-out and additional hours may cost more.
- **Multiple pets or longer stays:** ask about family rates, discounts and whether animals can stay together.

## How to choose the right service

If your cat is most comfortable in familiar surroundings, start by considering home visits. If they need extended human company, discuss boarding or a specialist hotel. For dogs, pay particular attention to the real walking schedule, time spent with a person, interactions with other animals and whether the overnight setup fits your dog's routine.

Before booking, ask who will care for your pet, how many animals stay at once, what updates you'll receive, what the quoted price covers, whether an introduction is possible and what happens if your pet needs extra attention.
